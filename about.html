Dawn and Night.

D&N is a fictional brand about the two ends of a training day. The early session before the world wakes, and the late one after it quiets.

The clothes are meant for both. Plain shapes, honest fabric, and a small palette drawn from forest, stone and sand. No loud logos. The work does the talking.

Three principles

Show up

Clothes that are ready when you are, at any hour.

Do the work

Fabric and fit tested for reps, not for photos.

Rest well

Soft layers for the part of training no one posts.
